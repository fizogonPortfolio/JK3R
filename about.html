WHO IS JK3R?

JK3R is the artist name of Jai Wilson, a musician from the UK whose work brings together rock, emo, goth and rap influences. Music is a way to explore ideas, communicate feelings and develop a sound that feels personal rather than fitting into one category.

Alongside writing and releasing music, Jai creates online content and continues to build his presence as an independent creative. This page is a central place to learn more about the artist, find his official platforms and get answers to common questions.
